04 — About

Hi, I'm Krishiv.

I'm a 2nd-year Electronics & Communication Engineering student at Jaypee Institute of Information Technology (JIIT), Noida, building AI and ML systems for Delhi's air quality and road safety — problems where software has to act on the physical world.

My flagship project, VAYUMitre, turns live CPCB air-quality data into spray-truck dispatch plans with XGBoost PM2.5 forecasts, and is incubated at BioNEST-JIIT. I also love the zero-to-one of building startups — which is why I co-founded Elmoak, a digital services startup building client websites and AI agent tools for business automation. I build and ship with AI coding tools like Claude Code, and I'm currently looking for a software or AI/ML internship.
